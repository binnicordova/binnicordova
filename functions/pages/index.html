Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of mobile projects are covered here?

Marketplace offline-first platforms at The Coca-Cola Company, enterprise sports streaming for NFL+ CTV reaching millions of users, secure banking for Itau Bank's 100K+ customers, ecommerce modernization at Platanitos with 1M+ downloads, and healthcare reporting systems deployed across 3,000+ hospitals and clinics for Gruppo GPI.

Which technologies do you use most often?

React Native and Expo (Managed and Bare) form the core, paired with TypeScript, Swift, and Kotlin/Java for native modules. On the backend I rely on GraphQL, REST APIs, AWS, Firebase, and Google Cloud, with Jest and Appium covering test automation across the React Native New Architecture (JSI, Fabric, TurboModules).

How do you optimize for high traffic and reliability?

Offline-first sync keeps apps usable on unreliable networks, while BFF service boundaries isolate business logic from the client. I profile production builds to catch memory and startup regressions, isolate native modules for security-sensitive flows, and run CI/CD through EAS and GitHub Actions so releases stay fast and stable at scale.

Can teams request architecture consulting?

Yes. I take on architecture advisory and fractional lead engagements for teams that need a senior second opinion on mobile strategy. Reach out via LinkedIn or the contact section with your product context, technical constraints, and delivery timeline, and I will respond with next steps and availability.

What are your top 3 React Native tips?

Use memoization with intention and avoid overusing it on low-capability devices, since excess memoization adds overhead too. Build native bridges as separate modules to improve implementation quality and maintainability. Use OTA updates to deliver urgent production fixes without waiting for full app-store release cycles.

Can you share a production bug you solved?

On the NFL+ CTV app, streaming to millions of users, I discovered a memory leak where repeated play and pause taps triggered broad rerenders across the screen. I resolved it by isolating player-control state, applying targeted memoization, stabilizing callbacks, and cleaning up lingering listeners, which cut wasted re-renders significantly.

Why use React Native and Expo for enterprise mobile delivery?

React Native with Expo enables fast cross-platform delivery across iOS and Android while still supporting secure native modules in Swift and Kotlin when product requirements demand hardware-level integrations like biometrics or encrypted storage. It helps teams reduce duplicate effort, share one codebase, and keep release velocity high without sacrificing quality or security.

With 7+ years delivering production mobile systems across fintech, media, banking, and healthcare, I am actively targeting senior mobile engineering roles in Canada, the USA, and Europe, and can start onboarding within a standard notice period.
